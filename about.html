About us关于我们

The foundation was established to honor our family’s values through compassionate service, especially to elderly people in rural China, and to inspire future generations to continue this legacy.基金会的成立，是为了以善意的服务传承我们的家族价值观，尤其关爱中国农村的老人，并激励后代延续这份传统。

Our mission我们的使命

To improve the lives of individuals and communities in need by providing charitable assistance, with emphasis on supporting elderly individuals in rural and underserved communities, including Guajing, China.通过慈善援助改善有需要的个人和社区的生活，重点支持农村和欠发达地区的老人，包括中国的瓜井。
